Privacy Policy

Personal Drive Mount is used only by its owner to access the owner's own files.

  • Data accessed through the Google APIs is used solely to display and sync the owner's files on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is collected, stored on any server, sold, or shared with third parties.
  • Access tokens and cached files are kept only on the owner's local device.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
